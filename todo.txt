[ ] 7.1 THE TALLBOY MECHANIC
    Location: Add "Stop N Rob" convenience store to Polonia (near Basilica).
    
    CORE CONCEPT:
    Satire of Milwaukee dudes walking around with paper-bagged tallboys at 10am.
    Everyone knows what's in the bag. The bag fools nobody.
    
    BASIC IMPLEMENTATION (V1):
    - Buy tallboy at Stop N Rob -> held in hand (not inventory)
    - Must carry paper bag visibly
    - Cannot put in backpack (it's a hand item)
    - 1-2 NPCs force you to open it (Mrs. Higgins?)
    - Once opened: must walk 3+ blocks AND talk to 5+ people before disposal
    - Effects: HP -2, Stress -15 (simple one-time)
    
    ENHANCED IMPLEMENTATION (V2 - FUTURE):
    The Milwaukee Curve - phased effects over time:
    
    Phase 1 - "The Purchase":
        - Fresh in bag, walking around
        - NPC reactions vary (Bobbie: respect, The Center: write-up)
    
    Phase 2 - "First Sips" (opening the bag):
        - Immediate: HP -2, Stress -5
        - Status: "Drinking in public"
    
    Phase 3 - "The Buzz" (after 2 blocks walked):
        - HP +0, Stress -15
        - The sweet spot - everything feels manageable
    
    Phase 4 - "Peak Drunk" (after talking to someone while buzzed):
        - HP +5, Stress -10
        - Overconfidence. You text your ex. You apply for jobs you're not qualified for.
    
    Phase 5 - "The Crash" (after 5 more blocks):
        - HP -5, Stress +20
        - Reality returns. The warmth fades. You remember your caseload.
    
    Phase 6 - "The Empty" (can now dispose):
        - "Recycle" or "Pour one out for the dead homies"
        - Return to normal, but with a slight headache
    
    TECHNICAL NOTES:
    - Requires `player.held_item` separate from inventory
    - Track `tallboy.blocks_walked` and `tallboy.people_talked`
    - State machine: FRESH -> SIPPING -> BUZZED -> PEAK -> CRASHING -> EMPTY
    - Consider second tallboy delaying the crash (authentic Milwaukee behavior)
